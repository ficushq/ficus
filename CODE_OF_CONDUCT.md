# Code of conduct

Ficus's community is for people who want to build and run autonomous agent
teams. We expect everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions
and any other project space, to be considerate, honest and constructive.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Respect maintainers' time: read the contributing guide, search before
  filing, and keep discussions on topic.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these rules and may
warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does so. Decisions are
made by the maintainers and are final.

## Reporting

Email **contact@ficus.sh**. Reports are handled privately and you will get
a reply. If the report concerns a maintainer, say so and it will be handled
by someone else.
